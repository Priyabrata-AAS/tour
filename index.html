Invoice Lifecycle
Status flow with auto-triggers
Draft › Sent › Viewed › Paid ⚡ · Overdue
  • Invoice sent → client notified via email + portal
  • 3 days before due → first reminder auto-sent
  • Due date passed → status → Overdue, rep alerted
  • 7 days overdue → second reminder + escalation to account manager
  • Late fee auto-applied based on client billing settings
  • Payment received → status → Paid, receipt auto-sent to client
  • Final payment received → project closure flow triggered

  • All client PII encrypted at rest (AES-256)

  • Client data deletion request flow (GDPR right to erasure)
